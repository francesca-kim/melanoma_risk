How these numbers were made. Gene-carrier risks come from a Bayesian hierarchical meta-analysis (a way of combining many studies while allowing them to disagree) of published melanoma studies, following Ruberu et al., Biometrics 2024. General-population risks come from SEER cancer registry data (US cancer statistics), 2000–2023. All risks account for the chance of dying from other causes first.

“Next 10 years” is the chance of getting melanoma in the next 10 years for someone who is alive and melanoma-free today. “By this age” is the chance of having had melanoma at some point by this age. The range in brackets is the 95% credible interval (the range where the true value most likely falls).

This is a research tool, not medical advice. Results are preliminary and do not include skin type, moles, sun exposure or family history. Please talk with a dermatologist or genetic counselor about your own risk.
